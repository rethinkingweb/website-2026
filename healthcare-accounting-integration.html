Introduction

A growing healthcare company was managing its books across two separate accounting systems, requiring staff to manually reconcile records between them. RethinkingWeb built a direct integration between the two platforms over a three-month engagement.

The result is a connected accounting workflow where transactions sync automatically and reporting draws from one consistent source.

Problem Statement

Financial data lived in two disconnected accounting systems, and reconciling them by hand was slow and error-prone as transaction volume grew. The company needed the two systems to stay in sync without manual intervention.

Solutions

RethinkingWeb built a direct integration between the company's accounting platforms. Key elements included:

Zoho & QuickBooks Integration

Direct API integration keeping both platforms in sync.

Automated Reconciliation

Transactions reconcile automatically instead of by hand.

Unified Financial Reporting

Reporting draws from one consistent, synced data source.

Data Accuracy Controls

Validation checks to catch sync errors before they compound.

Implementation

The engagement covered API integration design between the two accounting platforms, automated transaction sync and reconciliation logic, and the unified reporting layer, delivered over three months.

Results & Achievements

The company came away with a connected, reliable accounting workflow:

Automated Sync

Transactions flow between systems without manual re-entry.

Time Saved on Reconciliation

Staff no longer reconcile records by hand each cycle.

Consistent Reporting

Financial reports draw from a single, synced data source.

On-Time Delivery

The integration was delivered within the three-month timeline.

Conclusion

By connecting the company's accounting platforms directly, RethinkingWeb removed a recurring manual reconciliation burden and gave finance staff a single, reliable source of truth.
