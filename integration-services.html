The Basics

What Are Integration Services?

Integration services connect two or more applications so records — customers, orders, items, invoices, service jobs, candidates — stay consistent across systems without manual re-entry. It's how a Pipedrive deal becomes a Sage invoice, or a Google Workspace form response becomes a task in Monday.com, in minutes instead of days.

Most growing businesses run five, ten, sometimes twenty different systems across finance, sales, operations, and product. Every one of them is a potential silo. Integration turns that stack into a single operating picture, cuts data entry, and removes the errors that come with copying records by hand.

Methods

How Do We Integrate Different Systems?

Most integrations are built one of four ways: direct REST/API connections, webhooks for real-time events, scheduled file or batch transfers, and third-party iPaaS platforms that wrap these into pre-built connectors. The right choice depends on your systems, transaction volume, and how standard your requirement is.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
Direct APICustom code calling each system's REST/GraphQL APINon-standard workflows, high volumes, real-time needsRequires ongoing maintenance as APIs change
WebhooksEvent-driven triggers pushed from one system to anotherInstant sync on create/update eventsNeeds retry & failure handling built in
Scheduled batchFile exports or bulk API pulls on a timerLarge master-data syncs, legacy systems, EDINot real-time; timing conflicts need planning
iPaaS connectorsPre-built templates on platforms like Make, n8n, or CeligoStandard flows going live fastSubscription cost forever; templates bend only so far
Custom middlewarePurpose-built orchestration using the methods aboveMulti-system workflows, AI automationNeeds a partner who can maintain it (that's us)

The truth most vendors won't tell you: a pre-built connector is genuinely the right answer for many standard requirements. We'll tell you when it is, help you choose and configure it, and only build custom when your requirement demands it.

Pricing

How Much Does Integration Cost?

A typical integration costs between $2,500 and $25,000 to implement, depending on the approach. Templated iPaaS connectors sit at the low end plus a monthly platform subscription; fully custom builds sit at the high end with no recurring platform fee.

ApproachImplementationOngoing
Templated iPaaS connector$2,500 – $8,000$99 – $600/month platform subscription
iPaaS orchestration (Make, n8n)$4,000 – $12,000$50 – $300/month platform + support retainer
Custom API build$8,000 – $25,000+Support retainer only, no platform fee

Three factors move the number most: how many entities you sync (customers, items, orders, invoices), whether flows are one-way or bi-directional, and how much transformation logic sits between the systems. We quote fixed prices after a scoping call, so you know the cost before we write a line of configuration.
