The Basics

What Does This Integration Do?

Stripe processes the payment the instant a customer pays, but that event has to reach your books before it means anything to finance. Without a sync, someone is exporting Stripe's payout report and manually applying each charge against the right SAP Business One invoice — and untangling the Stripe processing fee from the gross amount every time.

This integration listens for Stripe webhook events — successful charges, refunds, disputes, and payouts — and applies them directly in B1: incoming payments against open invoices, credit memos for refunds, and a payout reconciliation that matches Stripe's bundled bank deposit back to the individual transactions inside it.

Data Flow

What Syncs, and Which Way

Most Stripe + B1 projects sync the same core events. We adjust field mappings and account splits to match your chart of accounts and currencies.

Charges

Stripe → SAP B1

Successful payments applied as incoming payments against open invoices

Refunds & Disputes

Stripe → SAP B1

Credit memos for refunds; disputes flagged against the original invoice

Payouts

Stripe → SAP B1

Bundled bank deposits matched line-by-line against underlying charges

Fees

Stripe → SAP B1

Processing fees split to a dedicated expense account, net-to-gross tie-out

Invoices

SAP B1 → Stripe

Open A/R invoices pushed as Stripe payment links or hosted invoices

Subscriptions

Stripe → SAP B1

Recurring billing cycles reflected as scheduled B1 invoices, where used

Methods

How We Build It

Stripe exposes webhooks for real-time events and a REST API for on-demand lookups. We pair these with the SAP B1 Service Layer or DI API depending on your B1 version.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
Stripe WebhooksReal-time event notifications (charge, refund, payout)Posting payments the moment they happenNeeds a reliable listener with retry handling
Stripe REST APIOn-demand lookups of charges, balance transactions, payoutsReconciliation runs, backfills, dispute detailRate limits on high-volume accounts
SAP B1 Service Layer / DI APIPosts incoming payments, credit memos, journal entriesApplying Stripe events directly to B1 documentsPayment means and account mapping must be set up correctly first
iPaaS connectorsPre-built templates (Zapier, Make, n8n)Simple one-way payment logging, going live fastFee-splitting and payout matching usually still need custom logic

Where a template usually falls short: generic automation tools can log a Stripe charge into B1, but matching a bundled payout back to dozens of individual transactions — with fees split out correctly — is where most templated builds stop and custom logic starts.
