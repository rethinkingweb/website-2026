The Basics

What Is Shopify + Amazon Integration?

Shopify-Amazon integration is the automated exchange of listings, orders, inventory, and settlement data between your Shopify store and your Amazon Seller Central account. It means a Shopify sale doesn't oversell your Amazon stock, an Amazon order shows up in the same order management view as your direct-to-consumer sales, and the money Amazon actually pays out reconciles cleanly against what your books expect.

The core risk in selling on both channels is inventory drift — the same SKU sold in two places with two separate stock counts, unless something keeps them synchronized. The second risk is data fragmentation: Amazon orders, returns, and settlement reports living entirely inside Seller Central while everything else runs through Shopify. A well-built integration solves both, using either the Amazon Selling Partner API directly or Shopify's own multi-channel listing tools depending on your catalog size and fulfillment model.

Methods

How Do You Connect Shopify to Amazon?

The right approach depends heavily on catalog size and whether you fulfill through FBA, FBM, or a mix — larger catalogs generally need the direct API; smaller ones are well served by Shopify's own tooling.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
Shopify's Amazon sales channelNative app connecting Shopify to Amazon for listing and order syncSmaller catalogs, straightforward FBM fulfillmentLimited flexibility for complex catalog or pricing rules
Amazon Selling Partner API (SP-API)Amazon's direct API for listings, orders, inventory, and reportsLarger catalogs, custom pricing or repricing logic, FBA-heavy sellersRequires SP-API developer approval and ongoing maintenance
iPaaS / multi-channel connectorPre-built templates syncing Shopify with Amazon and other marketplacesSellers on multiple marketplaces (Amazon, eBay, Walmart) wanting one viewRecurring subscription; template flexibility has limits
Custom middlewareOrchestration combining Shopify, Amazon SP-API, and your ERPHigh SKU counts, FBA/FBM mix, or settlement reconciliation into an ERPHighest build cost, justified at meaningful order volume

The truth most vendors won't tell you: if you're selling a small catalog through FBM, Shopify's own Amazon sales channel is often enough — we'll configure that correctly rather than push you toward a custom SP-API build you don't need yet.
