The Basics

What Is Shopify + NetSuite Integration?

Shopify-NetSuite integration is the automated exchange of orders, inventory, items, customers, and fulfillment data between your Shopify storefront and NetSuite's ERP. Instead of your team exporting CSVs or manually creating sales orders in NetSuite every time a Shopify order comes in, the integration does it the moment checkout completes — pulling stock levels, tax, and shipping details along with it.

NetSuite is usually the system of record once a merchant outgrows spreadsheets or a smaller accounting package: it holds the general ledger, the item master, and often warehouse and multi-entity operations. Shopify is the system of record for the buying experience. The integration is the bridge that keeps both truthful — inventory doesn't oversell, invoices match what the customer actually paid, and finance isn't reconciling two versions of the same order at month end.

Methods

How Do You Connect Shopify to NetSuite?

There isn't one official Shopify-NetSuite connector — there are several credible paths, and the right one depends on order volume, how many NetSuite entities (subsidiaries, locations) you run, and how standard your fulfillment flow is.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
NetSuite SuiteCommerce ConnectorNetSuite's own bundle for connecting external storefrontsNetSuite-centric teams wanting first-party supportLicensing cost; less flexible for Shopify-specific logic
iPaaS connector (Celigo, Alumio)Pre-built template mapping Shopify to NetSuite recordsStandard order-to-cash flows, fast go-liveRecurring subscription; customization has limits
SuiteScript / RESTlet + Shopify APICustom code on both sides, orchestrated by middlewareMulti-subsidiary, multi-currency, or unusual business rulesNeeds a partner who can maintain SuiteScript long-term
Middleware (Make, n8n, custom)Orchestration layer calling both APIs directlyCombining NetSuite with other systems (3PL, CRM) in one flowMore moving parts to monitor after go-live

The truth most vendors won't tell you: if your order volume is standard and your NetSuite setup is a single subsidiary, an iPaaS template is often the fastest, cheapest path — and we'll say so even though it means less build work for us.

A Shopify-NetSuite integration typically costs $4,000 to $30,000 to implement. iPaaS templates sit at the low end plus a monthly subscription; multi-subsidiary or multi-currency SuiteScript builds sit at the high end with no platform fee.
