The Basics

What Is Shopify + QuickBooks Integration?

Shopify-QuickBooks integration is the automated exchange of orders, invoices, payouts, fees, and sales tax between your Shopify store and QuickBooks Online. Rather than your bookkeeper manually creating an invoice for every order and later trying to match a lump-sum Shopify Payments deposit against dozens of individual sales, the integration books each transaction with the correct product, tax, discount, and fee breakdown as it happens.

The hard part of Shopify-QuickBooks accounting isn't the order itself — it's the payout. Shopify deposits a net amount after processing fees, and untangling that back into gross sales, fees, refunds, and tax by hand is where most bookkeeping errors and delayed month-ends come from. A properly built integration reconciles that automatically, so your P&L reflects what actually happened at checkout.

What Data Syncs Between Shopify & QuickBooks?

A Shopify-QuickBooks integration is mostly one-directional — from Shopify into QuickBooks — with tax and item settings occasionally flowing back for consistency.

Methods

How Do You Connect Shopify to QuickBooks?

QuickBooks Online has a large app ecosystem, so most Shopify-QuickBooks integrations use a proven app rather than custom code — the decision is which app matches your order volume and tax complexity.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
App Store connector (e.g. A2X, Synder)Purpose-built apps that summarize Shopify payouts into clean QuickBooks entriesMost merchants — proven, well-supported, handles the payout mathMonthly subscription tied to order volume tier
Native Shopify-QuickBooks channelDirect connection built into Shopify's adminVery simple stores wanting a no-cost basic syncLimited tax and fee handling for complex setups
iPaaS orchestration (Make, n8n)Custom workflow calling both APIs directlyNon-standard bookkeeping rules or multi-entity setupsNeeds a partner who understands both accounting and APIs
Custom middlewareBespoke integration for high-volume or multi-currency booksHigh transaction volume, multi-currency, or franchise/multi-entity accountingHighest build cost, justified only at scale

The truth most vendors won't tell you: for most single-currency Shopify stores, a proven app-store connector like A2X is genuinely the right call — we'll configure and validate it rather than build something custom you don't need.
