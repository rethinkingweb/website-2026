The Basics

What Is Shopify + Salesforce Integration?

Shopify-Salesforce integration is the automated exchange of customer, order, and engagement data between your Shopify store and Salesforce CRM. It means a sales rep opening a Salesforce account record sees that customer's full Shopify order history and lifetime value without switching tabs, and a support agent handling a case can see the exact order in question, its fulfillment status, and any prior returns.

Shopify holds the transactional truth — what was bought, when, and for how much. Salesforce holds the relationship truth — who the customer is to your business, what they've been told, and what deals or cases are open. Without integration, those two pictures live apart, and B2B or high-touch DTC teams end up making decisions on incomplete information or asking the customer to repeat themselves.

Methods

How Do You Connect Shopify to Salesforce?

The right integration path depends heavily on whether you're on Salesforce Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, or Commerce Cloud, and how real-time your sales team needs the data to be.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
Salesforce Commerce Cloud connectorsNative tooling when Salesforce itself powers commerce featuresTeams already invested in the Salesforce commerce ecosystemOverkill if you just need CRM visibility, not a storefront
AppExchange sync appPre-built packages syncing Shopify orders into Sales/Service CloudStandard customer-and-order visibility, fast go-liveRecurring subscription; limited to package's object mapping
Salesforce REST/Bulk API + Shopify webhooksCustom integration using both platforms' APIs directlyCustom objects, complex territory or approval logicNeeds ongoing Apex/Salesforce admin support
Middleware (Make, n8n, custom)Orchestration layer combining Shopify with Salesforce and other systemsMulti-system flows — e.g. Shopify + Salesforce + ERPMore moving parts to monitor after go-live

The truth most vendors won't tell you: if you just need order history and LTV visible on the Contact record, an AppExchange sync app is usually faster and cheaper than custom Apex — we'll say so even when it means less build work for us.

How Much Does Shopify + Salesforce Integration Cost?

A Shopify-Salesforce integration typically costs $3,500 to $22,000 to implement. AppExchange sync apps sit at the low end plus a monthly subscription; custom Apex-based builds sit at the high end.

ApproachImplementationOngoing
AppExchange sync app setup$3,500 – $8,000$50 – $400/month package subscription
Middleware orchestration (Make/n8n)$5,000 – $12,000$50 – $250/month platform + support retainer
Custom Apex / API build$9,000 – $22,000+Support retainer only, no platform fee

Custom Salesforce objects, complex territory or approval rules, and syncing to both Sales and Service Cloud are what typically push a project toward the custom end. We quote fixed prices after reviewing your Salesforce org.
