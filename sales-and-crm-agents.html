            Questions

            Answers before you ask.

            What is an AI sales agent, and how is it different from CRM workflow automation?

            Standard CRM automation follows fixed rules, such as sending the same email three days after a form fill. An AI sales agent reads the lead's details and replies, researches the company, writes a tailored follow-up, answers questions, updates the CRM and decides the next step within rules you set. It handles variation in the conversation and knows when to bring in a rep.

            Which CRMs can the agent work with?

            We build sales and CRM agents on Salesforce, Zoho CRM, Attio and Twenty, and connect them to your email, calendar, website forms and accounting tools. If you run a different CRM, tell us and we will scope the connection.

            Will the agent email prospects on its own?

            Only if you want it to. You can let the agent send from approved templates and tone guidelines, have it draft messages for a rep to approve, or mix both by lead type. It respects the unsubscribe and opt-out status recorded in your CRM, and every message is logged against the contact.

            How does the agent qualify and score leads?

            You define what a good lead looks like, such as company size, industry, region, budget and buying signals like a demo request or pricing page visit. The agent gathers those details from the form, public company information and the conversation, scores the lead against your criteria, and routes it to nurture, follow-up or a rep.

            When does a sales rep take over?

            You set the handover rules. Common triggers are a high lead score, a reply that shows buying intent, a pricing or contract question, or a named account. The rep gets an alert with a short brief, the full conversation and the CRM record, so they can pick up without re-asking questions.

            Can the agent clean up our existing CRM data?

            Yes. CRM agents can find and merge duplicates, fill missing fields, standardise company names and job titles, log activity from email and calls, and flag deals that have gone quiet. Changes follow rules you approve, and every edit is recorded so it can be reviewed.

            How much do sales and CRM agents cost, and how long do they take to launch?

            Cost depends on lead volume, the number of systems the agent connects to, the state of your CRM data and the level of ongoing support. A focused agent for one workflow, such as inbound lead follow-up, can often be piloted in a matter of weeks. After a short discovery call we give you a scoped proposal with a realistic range.

            How do we measure whether the agent is working?

            We agree the measures before launch, typically time to first follow-up, reply rate, meetings booked, lead-to-opportunity conversion and CRM data completeness. Your sales team reviews real conversations during the pilot, and we tune the rules and messaging from what we see.
