The Basics

What Does a Twenty + HubSpot Integration Do?

Campaigns and lead activity live in HubSpot, but deal context lives in the CRM — without integration, sales reps work leads with no idea which campaigns or emails actually moved them. A Twenty + HubSpot integration matches HubSpot contacts and companies against Twenty People and Company records, and keeps lead source, engagement, and pipeline stage current automatically.

HubSpot isn't a native Twenty connector, so this integration runs on the HubSpot API reading contacts, campaigns, and deals, paired with HubSpot webhooks for real-time events on form submissions, email engagement, and deal-stage changes.

On the Twenty side, we write matched records through the REST or GraphQL API, updating standard fields for simple cases or a custom Campaign/Engagement object where marketing and sales need deeper reporting.

Methods

How Do You Integrate HubSpot with Twenty?

HubSpot has no native Twenty connector, so every integration runs on the HubSpot API and webhooks. The choice is between event-driven webhook sync and scheduled polling, depending on how time-sensitive your lead and pipeline reporting needs to be.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
HubSpot Webhooks + Twenty APIReal-time push on deal, campaign, and engagement events into Twenty via REST or GraphQLReal-time lead-scoring and pipeline accuracyRequires a small always-on service to receive and verify webhook signatures
HubSpot API (scheduled sync)Periodic pulls of contacts and deal stagesLower-urgency reporting or nightly reconciliationNot real-time — hot-lead signals arrive with a delay
iPaaS templates (Zapier/Make)Pre-built HubSpot-to-CRM flow triggersSimple one-way status updates on a small contact baseCustom lead-scoring and Campaign objects outgrow the template quickly

The truth most vendors won't tell you: if you just need basic contact and deal-stage sync, Twenty's native sync is genuinely the right answer and costs nothing extra to run. We'll tell you when that's the case rather than pitching a custom build you don't need.
