The Basics

What Does a Twenty + Outlook Integration Do?

Sales and account teams live in their inbox, but a CRM is only useful if that activity ends up on the record. A Twenty + Outlook integration matches incoming and outgoing email addresses against your People records and logs the thread automatically, so anyone opening a Twenty record sees the full email history without anyone having to remember to CC or forward.

Twenty ships native Outlook and Microsoft Calendar sync via Microsoft Graph: once a workspace member connects their Microsoft 365 account, matched emails and meetings appear on the record with no middleware required. For most teams, our job is configuring that sync correctly — matching rules, which folders are excluded, and which fields calendar events map to.

Where teams need more — shared mailbox routing, custom matching against secondary email addresses, or writing calendar events back into Twenty as structured Meeting or Task objects — we build on top of Microsoft Graph and Twenty's REST or GraphQL API and webhooks to fill the gap.

Methods

How Do You Integrate Outlook with Twenty?

Most teams only need Twenty's native Outlook sync configured correctly. We reach for custom middleware on Microsoft Graph only when routing, matching, or write-back requirements go beyond it.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
Native Outlook & Calendar SyncTwenty's built-in per-user Microsoft 365 account connectionStandard email & meeting logging with no custom logicMatching rules and excluded folders need configuring per team
Microsoft Graph + Twenty REST/GraphQLCustom middleware reading Outlook and writing structured Twenty recordsShared mailboxes, secondary-address matching, custom Meeting/Task creationRequires app registration and OAuth consent — that's the trade-off for the extra control
Webhooks (Twenty side)Push notification when a Twenty record is created or updatedTriggering a downstream email (e.g. a welcome sequence) off a new recordStill fetch the full record over the API after the ping

The truth most vendors won't tell you: if you just need emails and meetings logged, Twenty's native sync is genuinely the right answer and costs nothing extra to run. We'll tell you when that's the case rather than pitching a custom build you don't need.
