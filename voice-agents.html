Under the hood

What happens in the second after a caller speaks.

Five steps run in real time, with your rules and permissions applied at every one.

  1. 1

    Hear

    Speech-to-text turns the caller's words into text, tuned for accents, noise and interruptions.

  2. 2

    Understand

    A language model works out intent, using RAG to check your policies, prices and records.

  3. 3

    Act

    Function calling books the slot, checks the order or updates the CRM through approved APIs.

  4. 4

    Speak

    Text-to-speech replies in a natural voice you choose, in a few hundred milliseconds.

  5. 5

    Record

    Transcript, summary and outcome are logged, so every call is reviewable and searchable.

Questions

Answers before you ask.

What is an AI voice agent, and how is it different from an IVR or phone tree?

An AI voice agent holds a real spoken conversation, understands what the caller wants, and completes the task in your systems. A phone tree only offers fixed menu options such as “press 1”. A voice agent can handle varied requests in natural language and knows when to transfer to a person.

Can it handle both inbound and outbound calls?

Yes. Inbound agents answer, qualify, book and resolve. Outbound agents make reminder, follow-up and callback calls on the schedule and volume you set, within your calling rules.

Which systems can a voice agent connect to?

We connect voice agents to your CRM, calendar, helpdesk, ERP and accounting tools, including Salesforce, NetSuite, SAP Business One, Zoho, QuickBooks and more. If you run something else, tell us and we will scope the connection.

What happens when the agent can't help the caller?

It transfers the call to a person with a summary of what was said and what was already done, so the caller does not repeat themselves. You set the triggers, such as unhappy callers, sensitive topics or low confidence.

How do you handle call recording, consent and privacy?

We build disclosure and consent steps into the call script, apply the recording rules for the regions you call, and limit the agent's access to only the data it needs. Every call is logged with a transcript for review. Your legal team should confirm requirements for your markets.

Can voice agents speak multiple languages and understand accents?

Modern speech models cover many languages and accents. We confirm coverage for your callers and test with real call samples before launch, then tune vocabulary such as product names and place names.

How do you stop a voice agent from giving wrong answers?

The agent answers from your approved data using retrieval, follows tightly written instructions, and can only take actions it has permission for. Confidence thresholds trigger a handoff instead of a guess, and every call is logged so errors can be found and fixed.

How much do AI voice agent development services cost, and how long do they take?

Cost depends on call volume, the number of systems connected, and the level of ongoing support. A focused single-workflow voice agent can often be piloted in a matter of weeks. After a short discovery call we give you a scoped proposal with a realistic range.
