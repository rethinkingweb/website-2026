Introduction

A wellness and lifestyle brand needed an online store that reflected its brand identity and could support recurring, subscription-style purchasing as the business grew. RethinkingWeb built the storefront on Shopify over a six-month engagement.

The result is a clean, brand-forward store built to support both one-off and repeat purchases.

Problem Statement

The brand didn't have an online storefront that matched its identity or supported the purchasing patterns customers expected, including repeat and subscription-style orders. It needed a store that could grow with the business without a platform switch down the line.

Solutions

RethinkingWeb built a custom Shopify storefront around the brand's identity. Key elements included:

Custom Shopify Storefront

A storefront design built around the brand's visual identity.

Subscription-Ready Setup

Store architecture built to support recurring purchases.

Brand-Focused UX

Product pages and navigation designed around the brand experience.

Mobile-Optimized Design

A storefront built to convert well on mobile devices.

Implementation

The engagement covered Shopify store setup and theme customization, product catalog structuring, subscription-ready configuration, and mobile-optimized UX design, delivered over six months.

Results & Achievements

The brand came away with a storefront built to scale:

Brand-Aligned Storefront

A store design that matches the brand's identity and voice.

Subscription-Ready

The platform supports recurring purchases as the business grows.

Mobile-First Experience

A storefront built to convert well across devices.

On-Time Delivery

The store was delivered within the six-month timeline.

Conclusion

By building a brand-forward, subscription-ready Shopify store, RethinkingWeb gave the wellness brand a storefront built to grow with its customer base.
