The Basics

What Does This Integration Do?

Moving a sales team off Salesforce is rarely a single cutover weekend. This integration covers both scenarios: a full one-time migration of accounts, contacts, deals, and activities into Zoho CRM, and an ongoing sync that keeps both systems current during a phased transition.

We map every custom field and object before touching production data, so the migrated records in Zoho carry the same structure — and the same history — your sales team relies on in Salesforce today.

Methods

How We Build It

Salesforce exposes a mature REST/SOAP API and Bulk API for large data volumes; Zoho exposes REST APIs and Deluge on the receiving side. A phased migration typically layers a temporary sync on top of a one-time bulk import.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
Salesforce Bulk APIHigh-volume batch export of Salesforce recordsOne-time migration of large data setsRequires careful field mapping before export
Salesforce REST APIStandard REST API for smaller or incremental pullsOngoing sync during a phased cutoverAPI call limits on lower Salesforce editions
Zoho REST API + DelugeZoho's APIs and native scripting for importsLoading migrated records into Zoho CRM cleanlyDeluge alone isn't built for very large bulk loads
Custom middlewarePurpose-built sync layer running during the transitionKeeping both CRMs current while teams migrate graduallyNeeds a partner who can maintain it (that's us)

The truth most vendors won't tell you: a rushed migration is the single biggest cause of lost deal history. We map every custom field and object first, then migrate — even when that means a slightly longer timeline.

Pricing

How Much Does It Cost?

A typical Zoho + Salesforce migration or sync costs between $3,000 and $20,000 to implement, depending on data volume and whether it's a one-time migration or an ongoing phased sync.

ApproachImplementationOngoing
One-time migration, standard objects$3,000 – $8,000None — migration is a one-time project
Migration with custom fields & objects$6,000 – $12,000Optional support retainer post-migration
Phased migration with live bi-directional sync$10,000 – $20,000+Support retainer for the duration of the transition

The number moves most on data volume, how many custom fields and objects Salesforce has accumulated, and whether you need a live sync during a phased cutover rather than a single migration event. We quote a fixed price after a scoping call.
