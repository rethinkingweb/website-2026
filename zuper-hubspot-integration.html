The Basics

What Does This Integration Do?

A sales team lives in HubSpot and a field crew lives in Zuper, and the handoff between them is usually a person copying a deal into a job by hand. Details get dropped, the address is retyped, and nobody in sales knows whether the work actually happened.

This integration turns a closed-won HubSpot deal — or a service ticket — into a Zuper customer and scheduled job the moment it qualifies, keeps contact details aligned both ways, and pushes job status, completion, and invoice state back onto the HubSpot record so the sales team sees the full picture.

Methods

How We Build It

HubSpot exposes a full REST API plus workflow webhooks, and Zuper ships a native HubSpot connector alongside its own REST API. The decision is how much logic sits between a deal stage and a dispatched job.

ApproachWhat it isBest forWatch out for
Zuper native HubSpot connectorPre-built marketplace integration inside ZuperStandard deal-to-job and status-back flowsCovers the supported field set only
HubSpot workflows & webhooksTrigger on deal stage or ticket changeFiring job creation at exactly the right momentWebhook delivery needs retry handling
Zuper REST APIDirect access to jobs, customers, invoicesCustom field mapping and job logicRequires developer resource on your side or ours
iPaaS (Zapier / Make / n8n)Orchestration wrapping both APIsMid-complexity flows without heavy codeSubscription cost; templates bend only so far

Where a template usually falls short: creating the job is the easy half. Deciding which deal stage triggers it, de-duplicating contacts that exist in both systems, and mapping job outcomes back to the right deal property is where the real mapping work starts.
